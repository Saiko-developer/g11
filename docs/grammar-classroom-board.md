# Grammar classroom supporting board

This is a presentation-only change for `teachingMethod: rule-first` Grammar classrooms. Narration, lesson objects, versions, publication hashes, progress and private audio-cache identities are unchanged. Reading, Writing and Self-study keep their existing boards/experiences.

The new board follows the actual teaching clip (`cursor`), rather than repeating the same segment note list across all four audio steps. It provides:

- A current-stage indicator and short focus statement.
- Formula slots with consistent colours for subjects, auxiliaries, verb forms and noun phrases. Optional suffixes such as `(+s/es)` stay together.
- Labelled sentence parts where independently authored display cues support the actual spoken example.
- Identifying vs extra-information and comma-placement comparisons for Grade 10 Unit 1.
- Short notes with highlighted grammar terms; longer source notes expand on request.
- A thinking question and a collapsed full transcript.

Grade 10 Unit 1 has 16 authored display cues corresponding to its unchanged 16 clips: foundations, shared referents, purpose, example, position, phrase boundaries, identifying information, extra information, punctuation, agreement and application. The other Grammar classrooms derive formulas and rules from existing reference data, selecting only formulas/rules present in the current spoken clip. The board does not invent word timings or claim word-by-word audio alignment.

Tests cover every clip across all 24 Grammar lessons, apposition cue alignment, formula token boundaries, role/comparison rendering, transcript reset and unchanged canonical hashes for all 50 existing classroom lessons. Browser checks exercise the real guest classroom's Next controls on desktop/mobile, including Grade 11 formula display. No paid AI/TTS calls or database changes are needed.
